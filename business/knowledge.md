Northwind Dental Clinic is a private dental practice at 14 Harbour Street.

Opening hours: Monday to Wednesday 09:00-17:00, Thursday 09:00-19:00, Friday 09:00-16:00, Saturday 10:00-13:00. Closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Prices: check-up £45, hygienist cleaning £65, teeth whitening consultation free, emergency appointment £80. White fillings start at £90. Teeth whitening treatment costs £295 after the free consultation.

Appointments last 30 minutes. New patients are welcome and do not need a referral.

Cancellation policy: appointments can be cancelled or moved free of charge up to 24 hours before. Later cancellations and missed appointments are charged £25. To cancel or move an appointment, customers need to speak to the reception team.

Payment: card and cash are accepted. Payment is taken on the day. Payment plans are available for treatments over £300.

Children: under-16s are seen with a parent or guardian. A child check-up costs £25.

Parking: there is free parking for patients behind the building. The clinic is step-free and wheelchair accessible.

Insurance: the clinic does not work with insurance companies directly, but provides an itemised receipt that customers can use to claim.
